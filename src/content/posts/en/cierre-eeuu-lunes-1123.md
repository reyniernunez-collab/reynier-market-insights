**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 23, 2026, 15:42 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session flipped last week on its head. Nvidia's board handed the CEO job back to Jensen Huang, after input from senior government officials, and the stock rocketed 31.5%, its biggest one-day gain since 2003. The semiconductor index added 14%, the Nasdaq 100 6.9% and the S&P 500 4.7%, snapping a seven-session losing streak; it would be its best day since April 2025 ⚠️ (single source). The VIX collapsed approx. 26% to 26.6. The striking part is that relief did not weaken the dollar: Treasuries sold off, mostly at the short end, and the DXY rose 0.5%. The flash US composite PMI slipped to 54.6 from 55.9, though its price gauges stay high, and the New York Fed's Williams stressed, right at the close, that there is still work to do on inflation running near 3.5%. Gold reclaimed $3,600 and oil fell.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,237 · bullish (+4.73%, snaps seven straight losses)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 26,746 · bullish (+6.86%)
- **Dow Jones:** 49,595 · bullish (+3.01%)
- **VIX:** 26.6 · bearish (-25.8%)
- **US10Y:** approx. 4.92–4.94% ⚠️ · neutral (+1 to +3 bp depending on the reading)
- **Gold:** $3,637 · bullish (+3.45%)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 94.7% ⚠️ (morning reading, single source); January approx. 62% ⚠️. Williams was the last major speech before the blackout ahead of Dec. 9.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 93.6% ⚠️ (morning reading); mixed euro-area PMIs did not change the picture.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 79% ⚠️ (morning reading); the yen barely moved despite the risk appetite.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 69% ⚠️ (morning reading), after softer-than-expected UK services.

**Risks:**

1. **Short US week:** Wall Street closes Thursday for Thanksgiving; with thinner liquidity and the VIX still above 25, swings can be sharp.
2. **PCE and GDP (Wednesday):** October PCE inflation and the second estimate of Q3 GDP will show whether the Fed needs to keep hiking.
3. **Nvidia is not settled:** the internal investigation and congressional inquiries remain open; any news could bring volatility back to the sector.
4. **Consumer confidence (Tuesday):** after a steep equity slide, a weak reading would test the resilient-economy story.
