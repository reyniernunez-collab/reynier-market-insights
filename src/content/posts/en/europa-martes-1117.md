**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 17, 2026, 08:07 CT

**Executive summary:** Tuesday's Europe session left the dollar firm: the Fed's Musalem spoke of "several" more hikes before spring, and the euro lost 1.10 (low 1.0985) despite a German ZEW far better than expected (35.6 vs 28.8). The Stoxx 600 posted its biggest drop in more than a week, with Deutsche Bank down more than 6% after a press report on suspicious payments, while French bonds rallied. Nasdaq 100 futures bounced approx. 0.5% after losing 6% over two sessions, waiting on Nvidia. At the New York open, October retail sales rose 0.2% (0.1% expected) and 0.6% excluding autos.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:40 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.6 · firm, near 52-week highs
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0990 · below 1.10; ignored the strong German data
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3190 · no reaction to mixed UK jobs data
- **USD/JPY:** 159.30 · the yen couldn't hold its Asia gains
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.82% ⚠️ · single source; front end under pressure
- **Brent:** $92.8 · higher as another tanker is attacked in Hormuz

**Central banks:**

- **Fed:** a December hike is fully priced; January approx. 69%, measured before Musalem.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 91% ⚠️ (other sources see it higher).
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 81% ⚠️ (prediction markets approx. 74%); unemployment 4.6% and pay growth 3.5%, both below forecasts.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 84%.

1. **Nvidia (after the close):** the stock's implied volatility is approx. 18%; beyond guidance, the market will watch China exposure after the new US limits.
2. **Fed:** Waller, Hammack and Williams speak today; the FOMC minutes land Wednesday (13:00 CT) and could reinforce the January bet.
3. **UK:** Wednesday's CPI (01:00 CT, 3.6% expected) arrives with BoE hike odds varying by source ⚠️; the +155,000 jobs figure is single-source ⚠️.
