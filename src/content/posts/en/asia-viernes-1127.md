**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 27, 2026, 1:58 AM CT

**Executive summary:** The Asia session ended the short week with the broadest risk rebound in weeks: regional equities gained approx. 1.8% and mainland China rose approx. 4% (the CSI 300 on track for its best day since September 2024) after renewed official support for the market, with Chinese chipmakers soaring approx. 13%. The Nikkei added approx. 2.1% and Hong Kong approx. 1.9%; Vietnam helped by signaling "much lower" tariffs. Tech regained momentum after Wednesday's scare, when the Nasdaq 100 briefly touched bear-market territory: its futures are up approx. 2.8% and S&P 500 futures approx. 1.7%, although doubts persist about the payoff from heavy AI spending. US Treasuries slipped (the 10-year yield up 5 bp to approx. 4.90%) and the dollar barely moved. Metals shone: silver jumped approx. 7%, platinum approx. 5% and gold as much as approx. 2.8%. The US trades a half-day session today after Thanksgiving.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 2:00 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.74 · flat despite the risk rebound
- **USD/JPY:** 159.33 · the yen doesn't react
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6667 · leads on China and copper
- **S&P 500 futures:** 7,315.75 · approx. +1.7%
- **Gold:** approx. $3,650–3,690 · strong gain ⚠️ (quotes differ)
- **Brent:** approx. $97.5 · Gulf tension ahead of OPEC+

**Central banks (next meeting):**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 98%, unchanged in 24 h; prediction markets agree.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 95%, up 2 points in 24 h ⚠️ (readings differ).
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 78%, up 7 points despite softer Tokyo CPI.
- **RBA (Dec.):** hold approx. 100%; unchanged.

**Risks:**

1. **European inflation (from 1:45 AM CT):** French and Spanish CPI and German unemployment will drive the euro; a hot reading would firm up ECB hike bets.
2. **Half-day liquidity:** the US closes early (12:00 PM CT), which can amplify moves on thin volume.
3. **Gulf and OPEC+:** attacks in the region keep crude supported and Sunday's meeting could move it; copper is up approx. 6% ⚠️ (single source).
4. **AI doubts:** the sustainability of the tech rebound remains in question and Korea warns of rising market volatility.
