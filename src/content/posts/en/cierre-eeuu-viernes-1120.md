**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 20, 2026, 15:40 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session ended the week with a broad selloff. The S&P 500 plunged 3.7% for a seventh straight loss and now sits more than 10% below its recent high, in correction territory. The Nasdaq 100 lost 5.6% and roughly 10.4% on the week, its worst weekly stretch since March 2020 ⚠️. Nvidia sank 14% on reports of a regulatory probe into its accounting ⚠️ (single source) and the semiconductor index fell 10.7%; AMD dropped 13%. The core question remains whether AI spending will deliver what it promised. Money ran to bonds and the yen: the US10Y slipped to approx. 4.95% ⚠️ and markets no longer fully price a December Fed hike ⚠️. Final Michigan sentiment came in at 51.5, as expected, with one-year inflation expectations easing to 3.7% from 4.7%. The VIX jumped to 35.9, while gold and bitcoin also fell.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 6,910 · bearish (-3.70%, seventh straight loss; approx. -8.6% on the week)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 25,029 · bearish (-5.61%)
- **Dow Jones:** 48,147 · bearish (-1.93%)
- **VIX:** 35.9 · bullish (+26.2%)
- **US10Y:** approx. 4.95% ⚠️ · bearish (-10 bp; another reading shows 4.91%)
- **Gold:** $3,524 · bearish (-4.4%)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** a quarter-point hike is no longer fully priced ⚠️ (single source); lower Michigan inflation expectations back that read.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 93% ⚠️ in the morning reading; bets were trimmed later (single source).
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 77.5% ⚠️ (87.5% in prediction markets); the yen is acting as a haven.
- **BoE (Feb.):** hike approx. 76%, lifted by strong UK retail sales.

**Risks:**

1. **Short US week:** Wall Street closes Thursday for Thanksgiving; with thinner liquidity and the VIX near 36, moves can be exaggerated.
2. **PCE and GDP (Wednesday):** October PCE inflation and the second estimate of Q3 GDP will test the idea that the Fed can pause its hikes.
3. **Nvidia and tech:** any news on the reported accounting probe ⚠️ could deepen or reverse the correction.
4. **Activity and confidence data:** the flash manufacturing PMI (Monday) and consumer confidence (Tuesday) will show whether the equity slide is hitting the economy.
