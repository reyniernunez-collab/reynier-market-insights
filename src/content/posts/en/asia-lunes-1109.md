**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 9, 2026, 01:55 CT

**Executive summary:** Monday's Asia session wins back much of Friday's plunge: the regional index rises approx. 1.9%, led by Taiwan (+4.9%) and Japan (+2.3%) after support measures from Taipei. The dollar gives ground across the board (DXY approx. −0.3%) and the kiwi is the strongest. Nasdaq futures slipped into the red before Europe on Nvidia headlines ⚠️. In China, CPI comes in slightly below expectations and producer prices above. The December BoJ hike climbs back in the odds.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 01:40 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 102.0 · softer (approx. −0.3%)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1170 · supported by a weak dollar
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3389 · near Friday's highs
- **USD/JPY:** 158.06 · no clear direction
- **Brent:** $96.6 · lower (approx. −1.7%)
- **US 2-year:** 4.51% · steady

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 77–78%.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 72–75%, +20 pp since Friday; another measure puts it near 39% ⚠️.
- **RBNZ (Dec.):** hike approx. 68–72%.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike between approx. 37% and 62% ⚠️, depending on the measure.
