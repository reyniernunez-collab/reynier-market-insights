**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 24, 2026, 08:12 CT

**Executive summary:** Tuesday's Europe session undid part of Monday's risk rebound: Nvidia is down close to 5% premarket after the boost from Huang's return, Nasdaq 100 futures are off approx. 1.7% and S&P 500 futures approx. 0.9%, as doubts about the payoff from AI spending move back to center stage. Treasuries rallied across the curve and the dollar weakened: the index slipped from 104.35 to 103.94 and EUR/USD gained about 55 pips from Asia, backed by a chorus of hawkish ECB members who see euro weakness as an inflation risk. In the UK, CBI retail sales surprised strongly (+9 vs −28 expected) and Germany's IFO came in line. Gold, copper and bitcoin are falling again, and Brent hovers near its lowest since August.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:34 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.94 · loses 104.20 and erases its Asia gains
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0958 · day high at 1.0961 on a tougher ECB
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3088 · recovers after CBI retail sales
- **USD/JPY:** 159.10 · the yen gains as futures fall
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.648% ⚠️ · single source; low at 4.629%
- **Brent (January):** $89.26 ⚠️ · quotes differ across sources

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec):** hike approx. 100%, fully priced; the debate is how many more in 2027.
- **ECB (Dec):** hike approx. 92%; several members push for more tightening and higher minimum reserves.
- **BoE (Dec):** hike approx. 80%; Greene also sounded hawkish.
- **BoC (Dec):** hike approx. 79% ⚠️ (stale reading, and other markets put it much lower); Macklem speaks at 10:30 CT.

**Risks:**

1. **US calendar:** consumer confidence at 09:00 CT (forecast 92.4) and a 5-year auction at 12:00 CT; a strong reading could put the dollar back on its feet.
2. **Wednesday inflation and GDP:** October PCE and GDP are out at 07:30 CT, on the eve of the holiday; the next test for both the dollar and tech.
