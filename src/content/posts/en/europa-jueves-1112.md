**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 12, 2026, 08:15 CT

**Executive summary:** The tech rebound stalled in Thursday's Europe session: Nvidia was down 6.8% premarket as its board weighs the future of its CEO, and Nasdaq 100 futures fell 0.8% (S&P 500, −0.3%). In the Stoxx 600, a rotation into consumer names offset weak tech. With trading back after Veterans Day, short-dated Treasuries fell again (2-year at 4.76%). The dollar was nearly flat near its highs and Brent hovered around $92.
