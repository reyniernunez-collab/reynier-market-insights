**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 18, 2026, 15:46 CT

**Executive summary:** Nvidia's post-earnings bounce faded in the New York session: the stock fell 2.9% and the S&P 500 posted a fifth straight loss (-0.23%), while the Dow shed 561 points (-1.1%). The Nasdaq 100 still rose 0.4%. Doubts about the durability of AI spending and Nvidia's leadership vacuum weighed. The FOMC minutes showed almost all members see further hikes as likely, and Waller backed considering a half-point move in December. Even so, the 10-year yield eased to approx. 5.07%, helped by falling crude. The dollar ended firm and gold slumped 3.5%.
