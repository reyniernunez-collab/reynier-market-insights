**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 10, 2026, 15:44 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session turned on a hotter-than-expected October CPI: 0.4% headline and 0.3% core, each a tenth above consensus (no annual rate, given missing data from last year). With Waller open to a half-point hike if core printed 0.3% and Logan calling for at least two more increases, the 2-year yield jumped approx. 20 bp and the 10-year touched 5%. The dollar had its best session since September, gold fell hard and stocks slipped, led by tech.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,521 · bearish (-0.4%)
- **Nasdaq Composite:** 25,067 · bearish (-0.7%)
- **Dollar index:** approx. 102.25 · bullish (+0.6%)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.115–1.116 · bearish (approx. -0.55%)
- **Gold:** approx. $4,030 · bearish (approx. -2.5%)
- **Brent:** $91.5–91.9 ⚠️ · bearish (between -0.6% and -1.2% depending on the reference)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** a 25 bp hike fully priced; 50 bp approx. 10% ⚠️ (single source).
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 81–83% (morning read).
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike between 42% and 51% ⚠️, depending on the measure.
