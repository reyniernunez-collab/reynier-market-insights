**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 16, 2026, 2:00 AM CT

**Executive summary:** The week opens in Asia with risk aversion. China's October data come in below expectations (retail sales 0.4% y/y, industry 4.8%, investment −8.1%) and Japan posts flat third-quarter GDP. Even so, a BoJ board member says hikes are "necessary now," and the yen trades as a haven: USD/JPY dips to 159.35. The Kospi falls approx. 3.6%, Taiwan approx. 4% and the Nikkei approx. 2.4%; Nasdaq futures slip approx. 1%. In Hormuz, two tankers are reported damaged by mines and a vessel was hit by a projectile, and Brent rises approx. 0.5%. The dollar stays firm, with the Fed's January meeting as the main driver.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 1:35 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.44 · firm (Asia range 103.36–103.49)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1016–1.1021 · Asia range 1.1014–1.1030

- **Brent:** $96.25 · Asia range 94.46–96.76 on Hormuz
