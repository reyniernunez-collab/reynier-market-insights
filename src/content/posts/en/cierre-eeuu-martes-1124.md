**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 24, 2026, 15:37 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session handed back part of Monday's AI rally. Nvidia slid 5.9%, the semiconductor index 7.9% and the Nasdaq 100 nearly 2%, amid fresh doubts about the viability and safety of the most advanced models. Beneath the surface, the market was healthier than the tech index suggests: the S&P 500 dipped just 0.5%, with most of its members higher, and the Dow gained 0.9% to close above 50,000. Consumer confidence surprised strongly, rising to 103.3 from 91.9, far above expectations and the highest since January 2025, with one-year inflation expectations still at 5.7%. Even so, the dollar did not recover from its European slide and the DXY ended the day down approx. 0.5%. New US tariffs on China, the EU and a dozen other economies are being discussed ⚠️ (awaiting official confirmation). Gold fell 2.7% and oil rose.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,201 · mildly bearish (-0.50%, positive breadth)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 26,214 · bearish (-1.99%)
- **Dow Jones:** 50,019 · bullish (+0.85%, above 50,000)
- **VIX:** 26.4 · neutral (-1.0%, still above 25)
- **US10Y:** approx. 4.90–4.93% ⚠️ · neutral (readings differ)
- **Gold:** $3,554 · bearish (-2.66%)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 100% (morning reading); March 2027 is still in doubt, with approx. 41% odds of a hold. Confidence this strong reinforces the case for more hikes ahead of PCE.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 92% ⚠️ (morning reading; another gauge puts it higher); the euro held its gains after hawkish European comments.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 80% (morning reading); sterling was among the day's strongest currencies.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 79% (morning reading); the yen did not benefit from the tech selloff.

**Risks:**

1. **PCE and GDP (Wednesday, 7:30 AM CT):** October PCE inflation and the Q3 GDP revision will test the view that the Fed must keep hiking.
2. **Tariffs:** if the announcement is confirmed ⚠️, currencies of the affected economies and equities could react sharply, although the overall rate would not exceed what current trade deals allow.
3. **AI remains fragile:** after two sessions of extreme moves in Nvidia and chipmakers, any sector news can swing the Nasdaq by several points.
4. **Thursday holiday:** Wall Street closes for Thanksgiving; with thinner liquidity and the VIX above 25, swings can be amplified.
