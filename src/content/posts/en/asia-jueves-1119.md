**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 19, 2026, 1:57 AM CT

**Executive summary:** Wall Street futures recovered during the Asia session after six straight days of losses: S&P 500 futures are up approx. 1.1% (7,420) and Nasdaq 100 futures approx. 1.9%. Two drivers: Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share, the largest on record, with early indications of an opening 22% to 26% higher ⚠️ (single source), and the Trump–Xi meeting at APEC ended on a conciliatory note (Xi called ties "stable and improving"; Trump spoke of "fair" tariffs and keeping Hormuz open). That relief didn't reach Asian stocks: Taiwan, the bellwether for artificial intelligence, slumped approx. 4% ⚠️ (single source) and Australia lost approx. 1%, although Japan (+0.7%) and Korea (+1%) gained. The dollar didn't weaken with the risk appetite: markets fully price the Fed's December hike. USD/JPY hit its Asia high at 159.71 while Ueda repeated that real rates must rise, with no verbal warnings from Tokyo. The AUD touched 0.6665 and bounced. Gold regained some ground (approx. $3,735) after falling more than 3% on Wednesday to its lowest in over a year, and Brent steadied near $92 amid drone strikes on Russian oil depots and Houthi missiles intercepted over Riyadh.

- **Dollar index:** 103.82 · firm despite the equity bounce (Asia range 103.77–103.97)

- **RBA (Dec.):** hold approx. 96% (from approx. 100%).

**Risks:**

1. **US data and Lagarde (7:30 AM CT):** jobless claims (198,000 expected) and the Philly Fed (20.6) could reinforce or cool bets on more hikes.
2. **Yen at 160:** a zone Japan watches closely; yen implied volatility is the only one rising in FX, and USD/JPY is moving less and less with the broad dollar.
3. **Tech:** S&P 500 implied volatility rises to 5.75% despite the bounce; Anthropic's debut will test whether appetite for artificial intelligence is real.
4. **Gulf and Russia:** Houthi missiles toward Riyadh and drones against Russian depots keep crude sensitive to any escalation.
