**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 20, 2026, 08:12 CT

**Executive summary:** Friday's Europe session extended the AI-driven selloff: the global equity index is down for a seventh straight session, its longest losing streak since September 2023, and the Stoxx 600 fell 1.3% led by tech (DAX approx. −2%). S&P 500 futures are down 0.7% and Nasdaq 100 futures 1.5%; Anthropic is off more than 7% premarket and every stock in the semiconductor index is lower. The haven was bonds, not the dollar: the US 2-year yield is down approx. 10 bp and the yen is the strongest currency. Copper hit its lowest level since March, gold lost $3,600 and Brent slipped toward $91.50. UK retail sales beat sharply, but sterling couldn't hold the bounce.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:37 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.7–103.85 ⚠️ · sources disagree; no lift despite the equity slide
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0981 · below 1.10, where large options expire
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3108 · gave back the retail-sales jump (Asia high 1.3122)
- **USD/JPY:** 158.74 · day's low, approx. 110 pips below the Asia high
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.68% ⚠️ · single source; approx. −10 bp on the day
- **Gold (futures):** $3,579 · approx. −2.9%, fifth straight decline

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Jan.):** hike approx. 65%; the drop in 2-year yields suggests the market is trimming that bet. Paulson called for more tightening.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 93%; some readings point to fewer hikes afterward ⚠️ (single source).
- **BoE (Feb.):** hike approx. 76%, up approx. 18 points after UK consumption (sales +0.7% vs 0.0% expected).
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 78% ⚠️ (prediction markets, approx. 88%).

**Risks:**

1. **Final Michigan (09:00 CT):** sentiment is expected at approx. 51.5–51.7 with 1-year inflation expectations near 4.2%; a firm print could lift yields and cap the yen. Cook speaks at 11:15 CT.
2. **AI financing:** SpaceX reportedly sold $32 billion in debt to buy chips, with some tranches above 8%, and European bond issuance has topped €2 trillion for the first time; more debt supply pressures valuations and yields.
3. **Losing streak:** the S&P 500 is heading for its worst month since April 2025 and Asia for its worst week since the pandemic; with no clear catalysts, any rebound hinges on tech.
4. **Gulf:** threats against Saudi ports that crude is still ignoring; an incident would revive Brent's risk premium.
