**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 10, 2026, 08:08 CT

**Executive summary:** Tuesday's Europe session turned on Waller: he backed a quarter-point hike in December and left room for a bigger move if core inflation runs hot. The dollar won back everything it gave up in Asia and the 2-year yield climbed ⚠️, while longer maturities fell. Brent dropped approx. 2% and briefly slipped below $90 on hopes for Middle East peace. The Stoxx 600 rose 0.8% on banks and industrials; Nasdaq futures are off approx. 0.3%.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 6:36 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 101.75 · recovers its Asia losses
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1217 · weaker, French risk premium
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3427 · gives up its Asia highs
- **USD/JPY:** 158.16 · firm
- **Brent:** $90.5 · lower (approx. −2%)
- **US 2-year:** 4.61% · higher ⚠️ (single source)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 91–92% after Waller ⚠️ (84% before); half point, approx. 5%.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 81–83%; Lane sees a hike as "prudent."
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike between 42% and 51% ⚠️ depending on the measure.
