**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 23, 2026, 1:56 AM CT

**Executive summary:** After Wall Street's roughest week in a long time, with the S&P 500 in a technical correction and the Nasdaq 100 posting its worst week in more than six years, the Asia session opened the week with a rebound in risk appetite. S&P 500 futures are up approx. 2.2% (7,084) and Nasdaq 100 futures approx. 3%, and Europe is set to open higher. With Tokyo closed for a holiday, the region moved on support measures: Korea is preparing a stabilization fund, China is cutting its minimum margin requirement and Taiwan is restricting short selling ⚠️ (single source). Taiwan bounced approx. 2.2% after its worst week since 1991, while the Kospi kept falling, between 2.6% and 3.3% ⚠️ (conflicting figures). What matters for currencies is that the haven trade is unwinding: the dollar index moved away from its 19-month high (from 103.85 to 103.60), the yen lost approx. 0.2% and Treasury futures snapped a five-day rally (US2Y 4.672% ⚠️, single source; the cash market is shut for the Japanese holiday). Notably, USD/JPY is rising with equities even as the dollar eases. Gold is recovering from its worst week in eight months: futures gain approx. 3.3% ($3,632) ⚠️ (spot is up between 2.3% and 2.6%). Bitcoin is up more than 1%, near $61,600 ⚠️ (single source). Brent swings between Libyan output cuts and Middle East tension. The AUD is held back after an Australian services PMI at a five-year low (46.2).

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 1:35 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.60 · easing with the rebound (Asia range 103.59–103.85)
- **USD/JPY:** 158.92 · yen losing haven demand (Asia range 158.79–159.06)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1005 · back above 1.10 (Asia range 1.0976–1.1007)
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3123 · firm, near its Asia high (1.3132)
- **Gold (futures):** $3,632 · rebound after the weekly drop ⚠️
- **Brent (January):** $91.97 · swinging between Libya and the Gulf (Asia range 91.57–93.12)

**Central banks (next meeting):**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 95%, down 4 points; January, hike approx. 62% ⚠️ (another gauge shows approx. 54%).

- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 69%, down 5 points ahead of the UK PMI.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 79% ⚠️ (another gauge shows approx. 87%).

**Risks:**

1. **Flash PMIs:** France and Germany (2:15–2:30 AM CT), UK (3:30 AM CT) and US (8:45 AM CT); weak services data could stall the euro's rebound.
2. **Tech fragility:** chip implied volatility jumped after Friday's Nvidia slump and S&P 500 implied volatility is up almost 1 point (6.58%); the rebound is not confirmed yet.
3. **Costly hedging:** implied volatility is rising across FX despite the relief, especially in the AUD, yen and franc.
4. **Geopolitics and the Fed:** Libya and the Gulf keep crude sensitive; Williams speaks at 3:00 PM CT.
