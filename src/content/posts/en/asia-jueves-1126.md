**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 26, 2026, 2:01 AM CT

**Executive summary:** The Asia session regained its appetite for artificial intelligence after Wednesday's late comeback in New York, when the Nasdaq 100 was briefly down 20% from its peak before erasing almost all of the day's loss. Asian equities rose approx. 1.2%, only their second positive day in 11 sessions, with Kioxia soaring approx. 14% in Tokyo on a deal with Nvidia and Korean chipmakers higher; the Nikkei gained approx. 0.9%. S&P 500 futures are up approx. 0.4% and European futures point to an opening gain of close to 2%. The dollar barely moved: the yen failed to benefit from a more hawkish BoJ, where Tamura talked about raising rates quickly, even by 50 bp if needed. Copper bounced more than 3% from 11-month lows, gold held on to its gains and crude extended its climb. Today is Thanksgiving: US stock and cash bond markets are closed, so liquidity will be thin.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 2:00 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.75 · flat, no haven demand
- **USD/JPY:** 159.17 · the yen ignores the BoJ's hawkish tone
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0977 · steady ahead of the ECB minutes
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6637 · supported by copper and equities
- **Gold:** approx. $3,570 · holds Wednesday's gains
- **Brent:** approx. $95 · extends a two-session advance ⚠️ (conflicting quotes)

**Central banks (next meeting):**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 98%, down 2 points in 24 h; prediction markets agree.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 71%, versus approx. 50% before Tamura's remarks ⚠️ (other estimates higher).
- **ECB (Dec. 17):** hike approx. 93%; today's minutes will test that bet.
- **RBA (Dec.):** hold approx. 100%; unchanged in 24 h.

**Risks:**

1. **ECB minutes (6:30 AM CT):** a hawkish tone would support the euro and European yields; a softer one would take that support away.
2. **Tokyo CPI (5:30 PM CT):** expected at 3.1% (prior 2.8%); a high reading would strengthen bets on a December BoJ hike.
3. **Tariffs:** China vows to respond to the US trade investigation while keeping talks open to extend the truce; the EU also rejects the US decision.
4. **Tech and geopolitics:** unconfirmed reports that Nvidia would curb shipments to Southeast Asia and the massive Russian attack on Kyiv could cool the rebound ⚠️ (unverified).
