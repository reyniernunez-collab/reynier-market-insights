**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 19, 2026, 08:09 CT

**Executive summary:** Thursday's Europe session revolved around Anthropic's listing, the largest IPO on record: early indications point to a debut approx. 27% above the offer price ⚠️, and Nasdaq 100 futures rose more than 2%, with the S&P 500 on track for its first gain in six sessions. The Stoxx 600 snapped a three-day losing streak. The dollar eased after rising in six of the last seven sessions, and the euro reached 1.1013 from its lowest level since April 2025, though it slipped back below 1.10. The exception was the yen: USD/JPY hit 159.97 with no warnings from Japan. Brent was flat near $92 and yields edged higher.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:33 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.7 · easing in Europe, touched 103.59
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0988 · tested 1.1013 but couldn't hold it
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3158 · sideways, waiting on the dollar
- **USD/JPY:** 159.94 · high 159.97, possible intervention zone
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.83% ⚠️ · single source; firm with Fed hikes priced
- **Brent (January):** $92.6 ⚠️ · single source; Hormuz mines vs more refined-product supply

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Jan.):** hike approx. 71% ⚠️ (prediction markets approx. 62%); December is taken as a given. The 2-year real rate, near its 2023–2024 highs, supports the dollar.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 83% ⚠️ (other sources approx. 97%); Nagel said a hike "would send a clear signal."
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 84%; Japan CPI is due at 17:30 CT.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 78%.

**Risks:**

1. **07:30 CT data and the Fed:** ahead of the New York open, jobless claims rose to 203,000 (above forecast), continuing claims hit 1.763 million (highest since August) and the Philly Fed plunged to 6.3 from 20.9, though its prices-paid gauge jumped to 58.2. Soft data with hot prices complicates the January bet; Hammack, Goolsbee, Barr and Barkin speak.
2. **Yen at 160:** a verbal warning from Japan could turn the pair sharply; Japanese CPI is due tonight.
3. **Chips:** TSMC and Samsung are reported to be halting shipments to China ⚠️ (single source); if Anthropic's debut disappoints, the rebound could unwind.
4. **Hormuz:** two tankers reportedly hit mines ⚠️ (single source); a new incident would revive the oil risk premium.
