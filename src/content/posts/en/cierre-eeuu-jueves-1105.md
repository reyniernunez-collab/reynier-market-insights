**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 5, 2026, 15:40 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session wiped out the post-election rally in one go. Fear that the new Democratic Congress will tighten AI regulation sank tech: **Nasdaq 100 −6.4%**, its worst session since March 2020, and chips **−12%**. The **VIX nearly doubled** and bonds rallied as a haven. The dollar closes flat, the yen gains and crude rises sharply despite the panic.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,353.73 · bearish (−4.4%)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 26,630 · very bearish (−6.4%)
- **VIX:** 28.18 · stress (+70%)
- **Dollar index:** 101.60 · flat
- **Brent:** approx. $98.7 · bullish (+4.3%)
- **Gold:** approx. $4,100 · firm (+0.7%)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec 9):** hike approx. 78–84% in the morning read; the session trimmed bets on more hikes (⚠️ no verified closing figure).
- **BoE (Dec):** another hike approx. 30–50% after today's 8–1 (⚠️ conflicting sources).
- **ECB (Dec 17):** hike approx. 86–89%.

**Risks:**

1. **Friday's jobs report (7:30 AM CT):** with equities falling, a weak print would reinforce the pullback in Fed hike bets.
2. **Spillover into the Asia session:** Korea and Japan already fell today on the same fear; a second round would push the yen higher.
3. **Hormuz:** crude is rising despite the risk-off; any verified incident adds inflation pressure.
