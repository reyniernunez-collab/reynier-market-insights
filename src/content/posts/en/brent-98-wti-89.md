## Detailed Analysis

### Data and cycle

Effective Fed funds 3.75-4.00%. Oct hike 53%, Dec **76%**, implied ~4.21%. ECB Oct **hold 57%**, Dec hike 95%. BoE Nov hike **81%**. BoC Oct 58% / Dec 72%. BoJ Oct hold **82%**, Dec hike 74%. RBA Sept 29 **85%**. RBNZ Oct **61%** (down from 73%). SNB Sept hold 95%.

### Geopolitics

Asia wrap: bonds higher and oil extending its drop on _US-Iran talks_. The market is pricing tactical de-escalation, not a permanent closure of Hormuz. The Houthi-Saudi Arabia risk remains a tail, not the base case.

### Policy

Divergence intact: Fed Dec 76% vs ECB Oct hold. The immediate G10 event is the RBA (85%). WTI's drop to 89 weakens the energy passthrough argument for Canada and, to a lesser extent, for Australia.

### Cross-asset correlations

**30d:** CL-EUR **0.69**; CL-GBP **0.75**; CL-AUD 0.42; CL-ES **-0.86**; EUR-ES -0.23; JPY-DXY **0.99**; EUR-AUD 0.83; Gold-Silver 0.98.

30d regime = _policy + risk-on in indices_; the oil-beta came back in EUR/GBP, not in stocks.

### Sentiment

Stocks at weekly highs; WTI broke 90. Bonds react to lower oil. The market is "walking on water": index risk-on coexisting with an energy liquidation.
