**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 13, 2026, 15:37 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session ended the week with another slide in chipmakers (-3.4%), overshadowing an October PPI that came in slightly softer than expected, though still the hottest since 2022. Nvidia lost 2% and is down approx. 13% on the week amid uncertainty over its succession; the company denied it is already interviewing candidates. Most S&P 500 members rose, but tech weighed more. Short-dated Treasuries outperformed longer ones, the dollar firmed and bitcoin lost $70,000.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,562 · bearish (-0.40%)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 27,944 · bearish (-1.22%)
- **Dow Jones:** 52,348 · neutral (+0.10%)
- **10-year yield:** approx. 5.14–5.15% · bullish (+3 to 5 bp)
- **Dollar index:** 103.4 · bullish (+0.2%)
- **Bitcoin:** $68,160 · bearish (-4.5%)

**Central banks (morning readings, before the PPI):**

- **Fed (Dec):** hike fully priced at 100%; January, approx. 72%. The PPI takes steam out of the case for a 50 bp hike.
- **ECB (Dec):** hike approx. 92%.
- **BoE (Dec):** hike between 72% and 78%, depending on the gauge.
- **BoJ (Dec):** hike between 84% and 92% ⚠️, depending on the gauge.
