**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 27, 2026, 8:08 AM CT

**Executive summary:** Friday's Europe session was defined by much softer-than-expected inflation: France's HICP fell to 3.2% (3.6% expected) and Spain's CPI to 4.1% (4.7% expected), as energy pressure eased. German unemployment rose by 9,000, twice the forecast, and the French economy shrank 0.2% in the third quarter. The euro fell on its own data, not on a stronger dollar. Risk appetite stayed strong after China's push in Asia: Nasdaq 100 futures rose approx. 2% and S&P 500 futures approx. 1.5%, copper gained as much as 6.5% and the Stoxx 600 is heading for its best week since July. Bonds slipped (the US 10-year yield near 4.9%) and Brent held near $97. Wall Street closes early today, at 12:00 PM CT.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 6:40 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.80 · firm, little movement
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0964 · mild selling bias after soft inflation; floor at 1.0956
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3128 · directionless; the 10-year gilt yield rises to 5.20%
- **USD/JPY:** 159.44 · close to the 160 area, where intervention risk grows
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6671 · supported by risk appetite and copper
- **Brent:** $96.64 ⚠️ · firm on Hormuz (single-source quote)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 98% priced, steady.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 95% ⚠️ (+2 points; another reading on the same panel shows 100%); despite soft inflation, one policymaker said two hikes "are not enough" and consumers' one-year inflation expectations rose to 3.5% (from 3.1%).
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 86% ⚠️ (other readings see it lower).
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 78%, up six points in 24 hours.

**Risks:**

1. **Hormuz and the Middle East:** a tanker struck a mine and attacks on energy infrastructure continue; Brent above $98 would revive demand for the dollar and hit risk currencies.
2. **Early-close liquidity:** with Wall Street closing at 12:00 PM CT, ranges narrow and a single headline can move more than usual; US data (goods trade balance and inventories) are low impact and the trade-balance consensus differs between calendars ⚠️.
3. **China and AI:** China's central bank set the yuan much weaker than expected, and there are reports of advanced Nvidia chips found at a data center in Shanghai; any stall in the tech rebound would hit the AUD.
4. **Weekend:** OPEC+ meeting (targets for the first quarter of 2027 expected unchanged), China PMI on Sunday (7:30 PM CT) and a review of France's credit rating after today's close.
