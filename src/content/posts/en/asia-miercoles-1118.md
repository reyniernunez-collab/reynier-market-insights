**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 18, 2026, 1:58 AM CT

**Executive summary:** Nvidia's results, with a better-than-expected sales outlook, sparked a rebound that Asia couldn't hold. The stock rose as much as approx. 4.8% after hours, but Nasdaq futures gave back nearly all of the gain and S&P 500 futures lost 7,380 (Asia low 7,363). Korea (+2%) and Japan (+0.3%) rose with chipmakers; Australia slipped approx. 0.7% and Hong Kong approx. 1% ⚠️ (single source). Gold lost $3,800 for the first time in more than a year, pressured by Fed officials arguing for more hikes and a US 10-year yield near 5.1%; silver fell more than 3%. Markets fully price a 25 bp hike in December and more than 80 bp of tightening through September 2027. The dollar index ended the Asia session at its high (103.74) and the AUD touched 0.6718, its lowest since January. As Europe opened, UK inflation came in as expected (3.6%; services 3.7% vs. 3.8% expected) and sterling eased. Brent fell despite fresh attacks in the Gulf, with focus on recovering supply and higher US inventories.
