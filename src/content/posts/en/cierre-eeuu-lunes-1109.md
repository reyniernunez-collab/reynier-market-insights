**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 9, 2026, 15:42 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session closed with stocks' best day since April: buyers returned to tech after the recent selloff and chipmakers jumped approx. 6.9%, even as Nvidia fell 4.3% on an inquiry involving its CEO. Brent plunged approx. 6% after Washington played down the odds of new strikes on Iran, easing inflation worries. The dollar slipped across the G10 despite firm yields. Tomorrow's CPI, the last before the December Fed meeting, takes over.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,549.94 · bullish (+2.35%)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 27,907.63 · bullish (+3.4%)
- **Dollar index:** 101.68 · bearish (approx. -0.6%)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1220–1.1225 · bullish (approx. +0.7%)
- **Brent:** $92.35 · bearish (approx. -6%)
- **Gold:** $4,153.50 · bullish (approx. +1.9%)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec):** hike approx. 76–77% (morning reading); CPI decides.
- **BoE (Dec):** hike between approx. 37% and 62% ⚠️, depending on the measure.
- **BoJ (Dec):** split, between 40% and 75% ⚠️, depending on the metric.
- **ECB:** presidency succession under discussion; limited impact on the euro.
