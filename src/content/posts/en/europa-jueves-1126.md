**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 26, 2026, 08:15 CT

**Executive summary:** Thursday's Europe session extended the tech rebound that began late in Wednesday's New York session: the Stoxx 600 gained 1.6%, led by miners, autos and tech, and S&P 500 futures rose approx. 0.7%. Copper bounced more than 2% from 11-month lows and bitcoin rose for a second day. The ECB minutes sounded hawkish (some members would have backed a hike already), but the euro barely reacted and the dollar stayed flat. European bonds slipped as safe-haven demand faded, while a projectile hitting a tanker in Hormuz kept Brent supported. The US is closed for Thanksgiving and futures close early, at 12:00 CT.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:35 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.75 · flat on holiday liquidity
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0976 · mild bullish tilt after the minutes, without breaking 1.0985
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3108 · little changed; the BoE warns about AI
- **USD/JPY:** 159.26 · the yen ignores the BoJ's hawkish tone
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6634 · copper can't lift it against the caution
- **Brent:** $95.09 ⚠️ · quotes vary widely across sources (91.01–95.09)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 98% priced, two points lower in 24 hours.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 93% ⚠️ (another reading puts it at 94%); hawkish minutes, with inflation at 2% only after 2028 and M3 money supply at 3.6%, above expectations.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 83% ⚠️ (five points lower; other readings see it lower); its financial committee warns about AI valuations and funding.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 71% ⚠️ (other markets see it higher), after Tamura spoke of fast hikes, even 50 bp.

**Risks:**

1. **Hormuz and Ukraine:** a tanker hit in the strait, two vessels struck by mines off Oman and new Russian attacks on Ukraine's power grid and grain ships in Odesa; an escalation would boost the dollar and the franc, with OPEC+ meeting this weekend.
2. **Holiday liquidity:** with the US closed and futures ending at 12:00 CT, ranges compress and any headline can move prices more than usual; the release of US trade data is unconfirmed ⚠️.
3. **Tokyo CPI (17:30 CT):** 3.1% expected; a reading of 3.4% or higher would support the yen and bets on a December BoJ hike.
4. **Trade and Europe:** China pushes back on the US trade probe but keeps talking about extending the truce; Brussels rejects the newly proposed tariffs, and Germany and Italy's rejection of the EU budget weighs on the euro. Friday brings French and Spanish CPI and German unemployment.
