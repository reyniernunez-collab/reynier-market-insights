**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 16, 2026, 08:16 CT

**Executive summary:** Monday's Europe session extended the tech selloff: Nasdaq 100 futures fell between 0.8% and 1.7% depending on the time ⚠️ and S&P 500 futures approx. 0.3%, while the Stoxx 600 was flat. Nvidia dropped more than 2% ahead of Tuesday's earnings, the first since Jensen Huang's exit; options price a move of approx. 7.8%. The dollar held firm with Fed hikes already priced, and the euro stayed stuck in a 1.1007–1.1033 range. Euro-zone industrial production plunged 1.5% year on year, far below expectations. Bitcoin lost $65,000 after falling approx. 12% last week.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:40 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.45 · firm, no sign of a turn
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1010 · pinned to the 1.1000–1.1007 floor that held in Asia and Europe
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3195 · steady
- **USD/JPY:** 159.45 · the yen didn't act as a haven despite falling stocks
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.83–4.86% ⚠️ · the short end stays under pressure
- **Brent:** $94.7 · eases despite Hormuz tension, with a well-supplied physical market

**Central banks:**

- **Fed:** December hike fully priced; January approx. 71%.
- **ECB (Dec):** hike approx. 93%; one policymaker already sees another hike in 2027 as reasonable.
- **BoE (Dec):** hike approx. 82%, five points higher than Friday.
- **BoJ (Dec):** hike approx. 84%, with the bank insisting hikes are needed.

**Risks:**

2. **Nvidia and chips:** earnings on Tuesday plus new pressure in the US to cut advanced-chip sales to China-linked firms.
3. **Canada:** CPI (7:30 CT) lands with BoC December hike odds varying widely by source ⚠️; the result isn't included here.
4. **Oil:** Brent showed a low near $92 in a single source ⚠️; Hormuz remains the risk of a sharp jump.
