> **Dallas, Texas — Oct 9, 2026, 2:00 AM CT:** The Asia session closes with oil correcting the war premium that pushed Brent to settle at $104.28 in New York. Trump ruled out an Iran strike before the November 3 midterms; the dollar and yields ease, gold rises and US futures reclaim ground after the OpenAI whipsaw. Asia equities finished mixed: Hong Kong up, the mainland down.

## Executive Summary

After Thursday's New York close — where Brent settled at **$104.28** (+$4.08) and WTI at **$91.49** (+$3.21) — Asia sees oil ease toward **~$102.95–103.5** (Brent) and **~$90.9** (WTI), depending on the time and reference. The catalyst is political: Trump said the US will **not attack Iran before November 3**, trimming the upside tail that had powered New York. S&P futures advance ~**0.3%** and Nasdaq ~**0.5%** after Thursday's chip selloff. Asia closes **mixed**: Hang Seng ~**+1.1%**, Shanghai ~**−1.2%**. The 10-year falls to ~**5.23%**, the DXY to ~**102.07** and gold marks futures near **$4,215** (spot ~$4,175–4,177). In rates, no central bank moves its next meeting by ~20 pp or more.

## Analysis

### Oil: Iran tail cut; Isaías still running

Thursday's New York close left Brent at **$104.28** and WTI at **$91.49**, well above the morning's implied daily range. In Asia that premium narrows: Brent trades between ~**$102.95** and ~**$103.5**, and WTI near **$90.9**, still inside the one-day range (±$3.0 / ±$2.6) versus the settle.

The brake is geopolitical. Trump wrote that the US will **not attack Iran before the November 3 midterms**. That does not remove Hormuz or the medium-term risk, but it does trim the immediate upside tail that had driven New York. The physical counterweight remains: Hurricane Isaías still has platforms shut in the Gulf. Shut-in production figures **conflict across sources**: some cite ~**511,000 b/d** (~25% of offshore) and others, in the Asia session, up to ~**1.3 mb/d** (~60%). Treat it as a conflicting range, not a single number.

### Equities: OpenAI flips the mood; Asia mixed

On Thursday Wall Street punished chipmakers (~−3.4%) after reports that OpenAI's annualized revenue sat ~$20 billion below previously indicated levels. In Asia, more constructive details on OpenAI's revenue projection (toward ~$70 billion annualized by year-end, led by enterprise) arrive, and US futures rebound. The trading takeaway is not a chip call — it is that the AI valuation narrative remains the week's volatile: one headline can move an entire session.

In Asia the picture is **mixed**, not a uniform rally. The Hang Seng rises ~**1.1%** while Shanghai falls ~**−1.2%**. A region-wide “+0.3%” appears in one reading, but what matters for trading is the HK–mainland divergence. Europe was set to open higher at the Asia close.

### Dollar, bonds and gold: yield relief

With oil easing and the Iran tail cut, the US 10-year falls to ~**5.23%** from the ~5.31% area Asia had marked on Thursday. The DXY softens to ~~**102.07**. Gold takes the cue: **futures** around **~~$4,215** and **spot** ~**$4,175–4,177** (different contract and timestamp; do not mix them without saying so). Gold's move is near or over the edge of the implied daily range (±$53), consistent with falling yields and a softer dollar.

### FX and Japan's household-spending print

Indicative levels near **1:35 AM CT**: EUR/USD ~**1.1226**, GBP/USD ~**1.3232**, USD/JPY ~**158.25**, DXY ~**102.07**, S&P futures ~**7,840**, VIX ~**15.41**. The euro reclaims the 1.1220–1.1230 area after Thursday's hit; sterling holds above 1.3200 after Bailey. The yen barely reacts to August household spending: **−3.1%** year-over-year (consensus −3.6%, better) and **+0.1%** month-over-month (consensus +0.5%, worse). Ninth consecutive month of y/y decline: the headline improves, the m/m worsens — a soft nuance for October's BoJ, where hike odds are already low (~10% in the main reading; other readings even lower).

China set the yuan midpoint at its strongest since February 2023 and injected 7-day repo liquidity; marginal support for CNH/AUD, without changing the global dollar bias.

### Scenarios for Europe and New York

- **If the Iran tone holds** (no strike before Nov 3), Brent has room to consolidate below New York's settle; Isaías remains the physical risk.

- **If Michigan lifts 1-year inflation**, the 10-year and the dollar can reclaim ground and cut gold's rebound.
