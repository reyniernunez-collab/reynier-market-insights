**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 12, 2026, 02:01 CT

**Executive summary:** Doubts over the future of Nvidia's chief executive, whose shares fell as much as approx. 10% after the New York close, cool the AI rally: in the Asia session Nasdaq 100 futures slip approx. 1.2% and S&P 500 futures approx. 0.5%, with Taiwan (−2.6%) leading the losses. Long-dated US bonds trade again and draw safe-haven buying; the 10-year hovers near 5%. The dollar sets fresh session highs near 103, the yen sits just shy of 160 and sterling holds up better than the euro after stronger-than-expected UK GDP.
