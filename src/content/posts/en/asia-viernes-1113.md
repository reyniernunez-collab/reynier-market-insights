**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 13, 2026, 01:54 CT

**Executive summary:** The Asia session digests Jensen Huang's definitive exit from Nvidia, with its finance chief as interim CEO, while waiting for US PPI. Asian stocks rose as much as approx. 1% and finish in the red; Nasdaq futures slip approx. 0.2%. Japanese and Australian yields jump after the US bond selloff, and the yen passes 160 for the first time since September, reviving the risk of intervention. Bitcoin drops below $70,000 and Brent keeps almost all of Thursday's jump.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 01:44 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.13 · firm at highs (Asia range 103.06–103.23)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1066 · Asia low 1.1051
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3234 · flat
- **USD/JPY:** 159.78 · touched 160.19 and pulled back
- **Gold:** $3,899 · pressured by yields
- **Brent:** $96.3 · holds Thursday's gain

**Central banks:**

- **Fed:** December hike approx. 98–100%; January rises to approx. 72%.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 92.5% (+14 pp in a day).
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 72–77%.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 84% ⚠️ another gauge shows 92%.
