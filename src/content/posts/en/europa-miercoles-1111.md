**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 11, 2026, 08:15 CT

**Executive summary:** Wednesday's Europe session brought back the appetite for AI: solid results and a hot IPO lifted Nasdaq 100 futures approx. 1.9% and S&P 500 futures approx. 0.9%.
