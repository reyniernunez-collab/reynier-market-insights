**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 11, 2026, 01:55 CT

**Executive summary:** Enthusiasm for artificial intelligence outweighs fears of a tougher Fed after the hot CPI: in the Asia session Nasdaq 100 futures rise approx. 1.5% and S&P 500 futures approx. 0.6%, led by the Kospi (+2.6%) and the Nikkei (+1.3%). US bonds remain under pressure after the biggest jump in the 2-year yield in 19 months, and they don't trade today for Veterans Day. The dollar ends Asia slightly above the New York close; sterling is the weakest. Brent rebounds above $92.
