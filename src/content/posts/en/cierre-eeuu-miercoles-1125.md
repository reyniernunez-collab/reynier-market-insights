**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 25, 2026, 15:45 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session had another dizzying day ahead of the holiday. The tech selloff briefly pushed the Nasdaq 100 to the edge of a bear market (20% below its high) and sent the S&P 500 down as much as approx. 3.2%, but buyers stepped in and the close was far calmer: the S&P 500 slipped 0.25%, the Nasdaq 100 0.64% and the Dow finished higher after erasing a 1,100-point drop. Nvidia fell again, 3.9%, and enthusiasm over its CEO's return keeps fading. The data gave the Fed no relief: core PCE rose to 3.3% year on year (a tenth above forecast), headline to 3.8%, third-quarter GDP was revised to 4.3% and jobless claims fell to 189,000. Even so, the 10-year Treasury yield fell for an eighth straight session on safe-haven demand. The US also proposed 20% tariffs on 14 partners, including China and the EU, that would not take effect before January. The dollar ended nearly flat, oil rose and the VIX closed above 28.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,183 · mildly bearish (-0.25%, well off the day's low)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 26,047 · bearish (-0.64%, brushed bear-market territory)
- **Dow Jones:** 50,183 · mildly bullish (+0.33%, erases a 1,100-point drop)
- **VIX:** 28.3 · bearish for risk (+7.3%, still above 25)
- **US10Y:** approx. 4.85–4.86% · lower yields (eighth straight decline)
- **Gold:** approx. $3,577–3,605 ⚠️ · bullish (readings differ; rises on haven demand)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 97–98% after PCE; January approx. 63% ⚠️ (single reading). Core PCE at 3.3%, GDP at 4.3% and claims near lows strengthen the case for more hikes; the Beige Book described slight growth and prices rising at a moderate pace.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 94% (morning reading); minutes of the last meeting are due Thursday.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 88% ⚠️ (morning reading; another gauge puts it lower).
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 70% ⚠️ (morning reading; another gauge puts it higher); the yen again ignored the equity slide.

**Risks:**

1. **Holiday and half session:** Wall Street is closed Thursday for Thanksgiving and trades only half a day Friday; with the VIX above 28 and thin liquidity, swings can be abrupt.
2. **Tariffs:** the proposed 20% duties on 14 partners open a comment period; any response from China or the EU could move currencies and stocks before a possible January start.
3. **AI remains fragile:** the Nasdaq 100 has already touched bear-market territory; losing last week's year-to-date lows could invite more selling.
4. **Fed and bonds:** Treasuries are rallying even though the data argue for more hikes; a hawkish Fed message could reverse the drop in yields and lift the dollar.
