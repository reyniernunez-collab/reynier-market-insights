**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 10, 2026, 02:01 CT

**Executive summary:** Tuesday's Asia session is quiet but tilted against the dollar: the DXY ends Asia at session lows (approx. 101.6, versus 101.7 at the New York close) while the euro and sterling post the day's highs. Regional data disappoint: China's trade surplus falls well short of forecasts, Australian business confidence slumps and Japan's services survey comes in weak. Brent keeps slipping. Pairs covered less than half of their implied range: the market is saving its ammunition for CPI.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 01:40 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 101.57 · at session lows
- **EUR/USD:** 1.1237 · at Asia highs
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3454 · at Asia highs
- **USD/JPY:** 157.97 · flat
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6923 · lagging on the data
- **Brent:** $91.5 · lower (approx. −1.2%)

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 80–84% ⚠️, depending on the measure.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike between approx. 41% and 51% ⚠️; a policymaker would not rule out a December move ⚠️.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 77%; another measure puts it near 50% ⚠️.
- **RBA (Dec.):** hike approx. 4%, all but ruled out.

**Risks:**

1. **US CPI (7:30 AM CT):** consensus is +0.3% m/m headline and +0.2% core, with no annual rate; the mix matters more than the headline.
2. **Waller (Fed) at 3:00 AM CT:** could shift December pricing before the data.
3. **Pre-CPI hedging:** with so many hikes already priced, the dollar could claw back ground before the release.
4. **Iran:** reports of threats to bases in the UK ⚠️; crude is ignoring them for now.
