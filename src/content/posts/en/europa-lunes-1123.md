**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 23, 2026, 08:12 CT

**Executive summary:** Monday's Europe session extended the risk rebound that began in Asia: Nasdaq 100 futures are up approx. 3% and S&P 500 futures between 2.4% and 2.9%, with Nvidia recovering ground premarket after last week's slump and the VIX easing to the 31 area. But the equity relief did not weaken the dollar: Treasuries sold off, especially at the short end, and the dollar index climbed back to 103.90 from approx. 103.60 in Asia. Flash PMIs painted an uneven picture: Germany at its highest since 2022, France in contraction and the eurozone slightly below forecast, as were UK services. Gold rose approx. 2.8% back above $3,600, while copper stays near its lowest since March, a sign that risk appetite is still fragile.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:34 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.90 · recovers what it gave up in Asia despite the equity rally
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0966 · loses 1.10; day low at 1.0957
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3098 · slips after weaker-than-expected UK services
- **USD/JPY:** 158.98 · flat; the yen is not weakening despite stocks
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.666% ⚠️ · single source; the 10-year at approx. 4.94%
- **Brent (January):** $91.17 · approx. −1.5% on the day

**Central banks:**

- **Fed:** December hike approx. 95%; January approx. 62% ⚠️ (other markets put it near 54%). Williams speaks at 15:00 CT.
- **ECB (Dec):** hike approx. 94%, practically fully priced.
- **BoE (Dec):** hike approx. 69%, about 5 points lower than Friday.
- **SNB (Dec):** hold approx. 91%; the franc gains despite doubts about a hike.

**Risks:**

1. **US calendar:** flash PMIs at 08:45 CT, a 2-year auction at 12:00 CT and Williams at 15:00 CT, the last major Fed speech before the blackout period; a hawkish tone would support January hike pricing.
2. **Wednesday inflation:** October core PCE is estimated at approx. 0.29% m/m; an upside surprise would strengthen the dollar.
3. **Chip fragility:** Nvidia's rebound follows a drop of more than 20%, with copper at lows; if tech slips again, risk aversion returns.
4. **Russia:** warnings about a possible nuclear-armed satellite and tensions in the Baltic; an escalation would favor the yen and the franc.
