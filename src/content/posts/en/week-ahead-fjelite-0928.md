## Executive Summary

The _Week Ahead_ video (US inflation, RBA, NFP and Iran) confirms the regime we already had: Iranian diplomacy pushes WTI lower (it reached ~89 on the FM–Witkoff meeting headline) and lifts risk; the US composite PMI at **58.4** (5-year high) and manufacturing at **52–57** vs 53.7 flip that bid toward USD and yields. Goldman in the clip: October hike ~70%, ~37 bp of tightening by year-end, 10-year UST at **5.11%**. The coming week will be decided by the **RBA (91%)**, **US CPI** and **NFP**. Hormuz remains the binary switch for Brent and the 30d EUR/GBP oil-beta (0.93 / 0.99).

## Detailed Analysis

### Data and cycle (what the video prioritizes)

The previous week's sequence: (1) an Iranian proposal to reopen traffic ~7 days after the embargo → weak WTI, SPX up, DXY down; (2) Trump: a 3-hour meeting with the Iranian delegation at UNGA → same pattern; (3) Iranian foreign minister–Witkoff → WTI <90 (~89); (4) flash PMI: manufacturing 57 vs 53.7, services 58.7 vs 55.8, composite 58.4 vs 55.3. From the PMI on, DXY and the 5-year rise, SPX under pressure. 5-year auction with a ~3 bp _tail_.

### Geopolitics

The video treats Iran as the first _intraweek_ driver, not as noise. Iran's condition: no full navigation through Hormuz under a blockade. A phased deal (already in FJ sources) = oil down / risk-on; a rejection = back to Brent >103–107.

### Policy

The clip's message: PMIs "are no longer irrelevant" because the bond market uses them to price _more hikes, not cuts_. That fits the Fed Oct **73%** / Dec **79%** from the tables cut. Monday's RBA is the immediate G10 event (91%). NFP and CPI are the test of whether the 58.4 PMI is validated in prices and jobs.

### Correlations and COT (no change to the map)

30d CL-EUR 0.93 / CL-GBP 0.99. COT Sept 22: EUR −52k and GBP −83k at 3-year extreme shorts; JPY spec longs −48k on the week. A EUR/GBP squeeze only if the Hormuz deal survives _and_ CPI/NFP don't rebuild the USD bid.

Expiries: EUR 1.1400 (€4.0bn), USD/JPY 160.50 / 158.25 / 156.75, AUD 0.7100, USDCAD 1.418. The RBA can invalidate the AUD pin.
