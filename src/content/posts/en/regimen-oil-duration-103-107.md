## Executive Summary

The regime is **oil + duration**. Brent rebounded to ~**103–107** and WTI to ~**93–95** after Iran repeated that Hormuz stays closed as long as sanctions and the blockade last. Westpac: Brent +4.2% to ~103; Dated Brent ~120. MUFG/DB: US composite PMI 58.4 (5-year high) and euro area PMI 53.1. 10-year UST at **5.11%** (highest since 2007); 2-year +14 bp. Fed October hike ~**70%**; December fully priced; terminal ~4.25%. The Senate rejected limiting war powers; at the same time there are talks of a phased Hormuz/blockade deal. USD bid; JPY near 160 (intervention risk, MUFG). The RBA stays at **91%**.

## Data and cycle

US flash PMI 58.4 and euro area 53.1. DB: ~37 bp of Fed tightening by year-end. Westpac: October ~70%, a full December hike. The 5-year auction cleared at 5.033% (highest since 2006), bid-to-cover 2.21 vs 2.33. 20–30y buyback: $4.078bn vs a $6bn maximum.

## Geopolitics

Two opposite signals on the same day: (1) Pezeshkian — Hormuz closed as long as there's a blockade; a rumored cap on US diesel exports (the Administration denies it and asks refiners for voluntary limits); (2) sources on a phased Hormuz deal in exchange for lifting the blockade. The Senate rejected curbing war powers. Crude is pricing the "Hormuz stays closed" scenario, not the deal.

## Policy

A global duration repricing (MUFG: it's not just France; OAT-Bund ~110 bp, the highest since 2012). The ECB also sees hike odds rising. RBA Sept 29 **91%**. BoJ: Japanese officials signal intervention with USD/JPY heading toward 160.

## Cross-asset correlations

The previous 30d already had CL-EUR **0.92** and CL-GBP **0.99**. The rebound to 103–107 reinforces that beta. CL-ES was still negative: stocks aren't buying the oil rally (wrap: bond vol keeps stocks in check). JPY-DXY 0.98 intact.
