**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 16, 2026, 15:45 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session opened the week with a deeper tech selloff: the semiconductor index plunged 5.3% and the S&P 500 posted its worst three-session drop since June. Nvidia remains center stage after Jensen Huang's exit; President Trump backed his return and downplayed national security concerns, while the US tightened remote access by Chinese companies to advanced AI chips. Warsh spoke about AI and productivity without signaling on rates. Bonds barely moved despite lower crude, and the VIX jumped almost 19%.
