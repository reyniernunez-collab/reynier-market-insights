**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 19, 2026, 15:45 CT

**Executive summary:** The futures jump sparked by Anthropic's IPO vanished during the New York session. The S&P 500 lost 2.0% for a sixth straight decline, its longest losing run since April 2024; more than 460 of its members fell and every megacap tech name closed lower. The Dow shed 1,101 points and ended near technical-correction territory. Anthropic gave back much of its early surge (approx. 17% above its offer price) and AMD tumbled 7.7% after a China-related short report that the company denies. The core doubt remains whether the massive, increasingly debt-funded AI spending will pay off. On data, the Philly Fed index plunged to 6.3 (consensus approx. 20) with prices paid at 58.2, and jobless claims rose to 203,000. The VIX jumped to 28.4, gold lost 2.8% and the dollar held firm.
