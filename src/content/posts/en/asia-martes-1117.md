**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 17, 2026, 1:56 AM CT

**Executive summary:** Asia sells stocks again. New US restrictions on access to advanced AI chips and servers, with licenses required to export them to data centers in several countries, hit regional tech: the Kospi falls approx. 2.6%, Taiwan approx. 2.3%, the ASX approx. 1.8% and the Nikkei approx. 1.3%. China's CSI 300 touches its lowest level since July 2025 before paring losses, while local chipmakers rise on the view that the curbs could speed demand for domestic alternatives. China's central bank fixes the yuan at its strongest since February 2023. The yen gains only slightly (USD/JPY touches 159.06) and the dollar stays firm near 103.5. RBA minutes flag upside inflation risks, but the AUD doesn't react. As Europe opens, UK jobs data come in mixed (unemployment 4.6% vs. 4.7% expected; wages ex-bonus 3.5% vs. 3.6%) and sterling barely moves. Nasdaq futures are flat ahead of Nvidia's earnings.

2. **US retail sales (7:30 AM CT):** 0.1% m/m expected and 0.4% for the core; a strong print would reinforce Fed hikes.
3. **Persian Gulf:** Iran says it is targeting a US radar in Bahrain while Oman and Iran discuss maritime security; crude is sensitive to any shift.
