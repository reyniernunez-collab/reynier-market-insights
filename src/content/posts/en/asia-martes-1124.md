**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 24, 2026, 1:57 AM CT

**Executive summary:** The Asia session posted its best day since July: the MSCI Asia Pacific index rose approx. 3.8%, snapping an eight-session losing streak that had pushed it close to bear-market territory. The driver is Huang's return at the helm of Nvidia, which had already given Wall Street its best session since April 2025 at Monday's New York close. Taiwan led with a gain of approx. 9% (TSMC almost 10%), Samsung and SK Hynix gained more than 8%, the Kospi rose more than 5% at one point and the Nikkei approx. 3%. Even so, momentum is fading: US futures are up just 0.2% and Europe is set for a flat open. In currencies, the rebound is not weakening the dollar: the index holds at 104.35 and USD/JPY hit 159.40 even though the BoJ leans toward a December hike. Williams's (Fed) hawkish tone, open to hiking at consecutive meetings, keeps a December hike fully priced. Brent eases approx. 0.5% with Hormuz on the radar ⚠️ (unconfirmed mine reports), Treasuries edge higher and gold holds Monday's gains.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 1:50 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 104.35 · firm despite risk appetite
- **USD/JPY:** 159.38 · the yen gets no relief (Asia high 159.40)
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0905 · flat ahead of the German IFO
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6628 · tracking risk without breaking out
- **Gold:** approx. $3,620–3,640 · holding the rebound ⚠️ (quotes differ)
- **Brent (January):** approx. $89 · easing calmly despite Hormuz ⚠️ (figures differ across contracts)

**Central banks (next meeting):**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 100%, up 4 points in 24 hours; markets price approx. 57 bp of hikes through March.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 79%, steady; not enough to cap USD/JPY.
- **RBA (Dec.):** hold approx. 96%; Australian CPI is due tonight.
- **BoK (Nov. 26):** the vast majority of economists expect a hike to 3.25%.

**Risks:**

1. **German IFO (3:00 AM CT):** an upside surprise could push the euro out of its range.
2. **Fragile rebound:** US futures are losing steam; consumer confidence (9:00 AM CT) and tomorrow's PCE could cool tech.
3. **Hormuz:** any confirmation of tanker strikes or US military action would bring volatility back to crude ⚠️ (unverified reports).
