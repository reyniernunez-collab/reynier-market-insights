**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 25, 2026, 1:56 AM CT

**Executive summary:** The Asia session gave back much of Tuesday's rebound: doubts about the durability of the artificial-intelligence cycle drove tech selling and the MSCI Asia Pacific index fell approx. 2.1%. Korea plunged more than 6% and Taiwan approx. 4.8%, while the Nikkei lost approx. 2.8%. Nasdaq 100 futures are down approx. 1.4% and S&P 500 futures approx. 0.8%, pointing to a second losing day in New York. The dollar rose against every G10 currency and Treasuries extended their gains: the 10-year yield fell approx. 4 bp to 4.89%. In Australia, October inflation surprised to the upside (headline 4.9%, trimmed mean 3.8%), yet the AUD fell as markets still see no move from the RBA. Crude eased despite fresh attacks on tankers near Hormuz, gold posted its seventh drop in eight sessions and the yuan hit its strongest level since 2022.

**Key levels (Asia close, approx. 1:45 AM CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 104.00 · rising as a haven
- **USD/JPY:** 159.05 · the yen barely benefits from the equity slide
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0940 · holding up better than risk currencies
- **AUD/USD:** 0.6570 · worst in the G10 despite hot CPI
- **Gold:** approx. $3,516 · seventh drop in eight sessions
- **Brent:** approx. $90 · easing despite Hormuz tension ⚠️ (quotes differ)

**Central banks (next meeting):**

- **Fed (Dec.):** hike approx. 100%, up 2 points in 24 hours; prediction markets agree.
- **RBA (Dec.):** hold approx. 100%; hot CPI does not change the outlook.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 70%, down 9 points in 24 hours ⚠️ (other estimates are higher).
- **ECB (Dec. 17):** hike approx. 94% ⚠️ (estimates differ).

**Risks:**

1. **US data (7:30 AM CT):** core PCE, GDP and durable goods arrive together; a hotter-than-expected PCE would reinforce the Fed.
2. **Tech:** if the Asia selloff spreads to New York, the AUD and the yen would stay driven by equities.
3. **Hormuz and the Red Sea:** new attacks on tankers and Saudi facilities could bring volatility back to crude ⚠️ (unverified claims).
4. **Tariffs:** US plans to raise tariffs on China, the EU and other countries weigh on risk, although Beijing offers talks in January.
