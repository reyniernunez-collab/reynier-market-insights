## Executive Summary

Oil broke below 100: Brent **99.35** and WTI **94.69**. Crude's 30d beta with EUR stabilized at 0.52 and with ES at -0.81: the supply shock no longer drives European FX. Stocks keep going (ES 7829, NQ 30779); JPM describes a _broader_ tech rally with "geopolitical optimism". The Fed prices a Dec hike at **78%**. The ECB's October moved to a **53% hold**. Oil falls on Saudi cargoes through Hormuz and hopes for a US-China summit (GS). The Houthi-Saudi Arabia risk hasn't disappeared, but the market no longer assigns it the 110 scenario. "Geopolitics and China in focus".

### Policy

Short-term Fed vs ECB divergence (Dec vs an October hold). The RBA is the G10 event of the week. Lower oil eases the inflation passthrough, but Fed pricing isn't softening (Dec 78%).
