**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 18, 2026, 08:14 CT

**Executive summary:** Wednesday's Europe session combined a stock rebound with a stronger dollar. Nvidia's outlook, with revenue growth through 2028, eased some of the fear around the AI cycle: S&P 500 futures rose approx. 0.4% and Nasdaq 100 futures approx. 0.9%, with tech the best Stoxx 600 sector and Samsung and SK Hynix higher in Korea. At the same time, Waller (Fed) said inflation is broadening and called for more aggressive action: the 2-year yield rose and the DXY hit its high of the day. The euro ignored higher-than-expected core inflation (2.6%), and gold fell approx. 2.5%, below $3,800 for the first time this year.

**Key levels (Europe close, approx. 06:40 CT):**

- **Dollar index:** 103.8 · day's high after Waller
- **EUR/USD:** 1.0966 · below Tuesday's New York low
- **GBP/USD:** 1.3160 · no reaction to very high producer input prices
- **USD/JPY:** 159.41 · rising with US yields, close to 160
- **US 2-year yield:** 4.82% ⚠️ · single source; higher after Waller
- **Brent (January):** $93.1 ⚠️ · choppy between supply and another tanker hit in Hormuz

**Central banks:**

- **Fed (Jan.):** hike approx. 72%, measured before Waller ⚠️ (prediction markets approx. 66%); FOMC minutes at 13:00 CT.
- **ECB (Dec.):** hike approx. 89% ⚠️ (other sources above 95%); Vujcic sees a December hike as "very likely".
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike approx. 80% ⚠️ (prediction markets approx. 73%); October CPI at 3.6%, as expected.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 84%; Ueda speaks tonight.
