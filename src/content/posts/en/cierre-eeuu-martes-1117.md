**REYNIER MARKET INSIGHTS**

Dallas, Texas — Nov 17, 2026, 15:45 CT

**Executive summary:** The New York session delivered another day of selling: the Dow lost 849 points (-1.6%), dragged by software, with Salesforce sinking 9.5% two days before Anthropic's market debut. October retail sales rose 0.2% (0.6% ex autos), above forecasts, and Musalem called for "several" more hikes, yet bonds closed with slightly lower yields. The dollar hit its highest level in 19 months before fading. After the close, Nvidia beat on revenue and guidance, and its shares are up approx. 2-3% after hours.

**Key levels (New York close):**

- **S&P 500:** 7,340 · bearish (-1.03%)
- **Nasdaq 100:** 27,002 · bearish (-0.72%)
- **Dow Jones:** 50,759 · bearish (-1.64%)
- **10-year Treasury:** 5.10–5.11% · neutral (-1 to -2 bp)
- **Dollar index:** 103.5 · neutral (unchanged after touching 103.78)
- **Brent:** $92.9 · bullish (+0.5%)

**Central banks (morning readings):**

- **Fed:** December hike fully priced; January approx. 69% before Musalem spoke. FOMC minutes arrive Wednesday.
- **BoE (Dec.):** hike between 73.5% and 81% ⚠️, depending on the measure; Wednesday's UK CPI decides.
- **BoJ (Dec.):** hike approx. 84.5%; the yen stays near the 160 zone.
- **BoC (Dec.):** hike between 44.5% and 49% ⚠️, after Monday's CPI.
